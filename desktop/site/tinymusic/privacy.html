隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 18 日

一句话：TinyMusic iOS 不收集你的任何数据。没有账号、没有曲库、没有广告，也没有第三方 SDK；App 只跟你自己的 S3 存储和 Apple 的 iCloud 服务通信。

我们不收集什么

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号、位置、通讯录、相册或广告标识符。
  • 不接入第三方统计、崩溃上报或广告 SDK，不做用户画像。
  • 不上传你的播放记录、收藏、歌单或缓存文件。
  • 没有账号系统，也没有在线曲库、搜索或推荐。

保存在你设备上的数据

  • S3 凭据：Access Key / Secret Key 只写入 iOS Keychain，不写进本机的设置文件；开启 iCloud 钥匙串时，可能通过 Apple 的端到端加密同步到你自己的其他设备。
  • 播放器设置：S3 endpoint、region、bucket、prefix、缓存上限、仅 Wi-Fi、EQ 等非敏感设置保存在本机，可能通过 iCloud Key-Value Storage 在你自己的设备间同步。
  • 离线缓存：音频、歌词、封面只存在 App 沙盒里，已排除 iCloud 备份，不能导出或分享，也不会出现在「文件」App 中。
  • 收藏：默认写到你 S3 桶里的 favorites.json；密钥只读时退回本机，不写桶。

网络请求

App 只连接你在设置里填写的 S3 endpoint，以及 Apple 提供的 iCloud / Keychain 服务。音频、歌词、封面和收藏直接与你的存储服务交换，不经过 TinyMusic 的中转服务器。App 内没有统计、广告或崩溃上报请求。

第三方服务

没有集成任何第三方 SDK。AWS S3、Cloudflare R2、MinIO 等存储服务由各自的提供方运营，受其条款与隐私政策约束；TinyMusic 与这些服务没有隶属关系。

你的控制

你可以在设置里断开 S3、清除缓存，或在系统里删除 App 以移除本机数据。桶里的 favorites.json 需要你在自己的存储服务中删除。

儿童隐私

App 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童，也不收集年龄信息。

政策变更

如果隐私实践有变化，本页会更新并修改上方日期；重大变化会在 App 更新说明中提示。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In one sentence: TinyMusic for iOS collects no data about you. No accounts, no catalogue, no ads, no third-party SDKs. The app talks only to your own S3 storage and Apple's iCloud services.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photo library or advertising identifier.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or ad SDKs, and no user profiling.
  • No uploading of your play history, favourites, playlists or cached files.
  • No account system, and no online catalogue, search or recommendations.

Data stored on your device

  • S3 credentials: Access Key / Secret Key are written only to the iOS Keychain, never to a settings file on disk. With iCloud Keychain on, they may sync to your other devices through Apple's end-to-end encrypted keychain.
  • Player settings: non-sensitive settings — S3 endpoint, region, bucket, prefix, cache cap, Wi-Fi only, EQ — are stored on this device and may sync between your own devices via iCloud Key-Value Storage.
  • Offline cache: audio, lyrics and covers live inside the app sandbox, are excluded from iCloud backup, cannot be exported or shared, and never appear in the Files app.
  • Favourites: by default written to favorites.json in your S3 bucket; with a read-only key they stay on this device and the bucket is left untouched.

Network requests

The app connects only to the S3 endpoint you enter in Settings and to Apple's iCloud / Keychain services. Audio, lyrics, covers and favourites are exchanged directly with your storage provider and never pass through a TinyMusic relay. There are no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting requests.

Third-party services

No third-party SDKs are integrated. AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, MinIO and similar services are run by their own providers under their own terms and privacy policies; TinyMusic is not affiliated with them.

Your controls

You can disconnect S3 and clear the cache in Settings, or delete the app to remove on-device data. favorites.json in your bucket has to be deleted through your own storage service.

Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, children included, and collects no age information.

Changes to this policy

If our privacy practices change, this page will be updated along with the date above; significant changes will be called out in the app's release notes.
