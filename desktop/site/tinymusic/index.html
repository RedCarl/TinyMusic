TinyMusic
桌面版Desktop app

iOS 18+ · 1.0 · SwiftUI

只播你自己的歌。Plays your own music.

TinyMusic iOS 是一个只播放你自己 S3 兼容存储内容的本地播放器。没有在线曲库、搜索、平台下载或账号登录。 TinyMusic for iOS is a local player that plays only what lives in your own S3-compatible storage. No online catalogue, no search, no platform downloads, no sign-in.

  • S3 兼容存储浏览：AWS S3、Cloudflare R2、MinIO 等。S3-compatible browsing: AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, MinIO and friends.
  • AVPlayer 直连预签名 URL 流式播放，支持后台音频与锁屏控制。Streaming through AVPlayer against presigned URLs, with background audio and lock-screen controls.
  • 把指定歌曲或整张歌单离线缓存，断网也能听。Cache individual tracks or a whole playlist for offline listening.
  • 收藏写回桶里的 favorites.json；只读密钥时退回本机。Favourites are written back to favorites.json in your bucket, and stay on-device with a read-only key.
  • 10 段 EQ、12 套预设与前级增益。10-band EQ with 12 presets and pre-gain.
  • iCloud 同步 S3 配置与 EQ；密钥走 iCloud 钥匙串。iCloud syncs your S3 settings and EQ; keys ride in iCloud Keychain.
  • Liquid Glass 界面，浅色与深色跟随系统。Liquid Glass interface that follows the system light and dark appearance.

它不提供曲库。TinyMusic 不提供在线曲库、搜索、推荐、平台下载或账号登录，只播放你在自己的存储里已经拥有或有权使用的内容。There is no catalogue. TinyMusic offers no online library, search, recommendations, platform downloads or sign-in. It plays only what you already own or are allowed to use in your own storage.
