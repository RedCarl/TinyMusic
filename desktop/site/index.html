一个 4.5 MB 的桌面音乐客户端。搜索、试听、下载、歌词、网盘都在一个窗口里；拿不到的歌会直接置灰，而不是让你盯着「正在搜索」。 A 4.5 MB desktop music client. Search, preview, download, lyrics and cloud storage all live in one window; tracks that can't be fetched are greyed out instead of leaving you staring at "Searching…".

能做什么What it does

找歌、听歌、把它们留下来，都在同一个窗口里。Finding, playing and keeping your music — all in one window.

搜索与浏览Search and browse

歌曲、专辑、歌手、歌单一起搜，结果按热度排序。歌手页给头像、简介和专辑，点封面直接进专辑整张播放。Songs, albums, artists and playlists in one search, ranked by popularity. Artist pages carry the photo, bio and discography; tap a cover to play the whole album.

播放与歌词Playback and lyrics

悬浮播放器管随机、循环、音量和队列；全屏歌词逐行高亮，点任意一句就从那里开始播。重开应用会恢复上次的曲目和进度，但不会突然出声。A floating player handles shuffle, repeat, volume and the queue. Full-screen lyrics highlight line by line, and any line jumps there. Reopening restores your last track and position without suddenly making noise.

音质Audio quality

标准 128k、高品质 320k、无损 FLAC。能不能播在点之前就判断好，没无损的曲目直接禁用；文件实际被降级了也会标出来。128k standard, 320k high quality and lossless FLAC. Availability is decided before you press play, tracks without lossless disable the option, and a downgraded file is labelled as such.

10 段均衡器10-band equaliser

32 Hz – 16 kHz 十段图形均衡，实时作用于当前播放；12 种预设加上前级增益，手动调过会自动记为「自定义」并保存。A 10-band graphic EQ from 32 Hz to 16 kHz that applies live. Twelve presets plus pre-gain; manual tweaks are saved as "Custom".

下载与网盘Downloads and cloud storage

下载时把封面和歌词一起带上，专辑、歌单、搜索结果都能「下载全部」；也可以直接传进你自己的 S3 网盘（AWS S3、MinIO、R2），「喜欢」就是桶里的一份 favorites.json。Downloads bring the cover and lyrics along, and albums, playlists and search results all support "download all". Files can also go straight into your own S3-compatible bucket (AWS S3, MinIO, R2), where favourites live as a favorites.json manifest.

托盘与后台Tray and background

关窗口默认收进系统托盘，播放不中断；网盘导入这类任务在后台排队跑，不用守着窗口。主题跟随系统，也能手动指定。Closing the window tucks the app into the tray and playback keeps going. Jobs such as cloud imports queue up in the background. The theme follows the system, or you can pin it.
